# SmartKart Sales & Profitability Analysis

A personal Data Analyst practice project built using Microsoft Excel and synthetic retail data.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes SmartKart's sales and profitability performance across products, categories, regions, customers, discounts, shipping modes, returns, and monthly trends.

The main objective was to take raw transactional data, perform analysis, identify business insights, and build an interactive Excel dashboard to present the results.

This is a personal practice project. The dataset is synthetic and was created for learning and analytical practice purposes.

## Business Objectives

The analysis was focused on answering the following business questions:

1. How is SmartKart performing overall in terms of Sales, Profit, Profit Margin, and Quantity?
2. Which categories and products are driving the most Sales and Profit, and which are underperforming?
3. Which regions contribute the most to Sales and Profit, and are there significant differences in profitability across locations?
4. Which customer segments contribute the most to Sales and Profit, and how does purchasing behavior differ across customer groups?
5. How does discounting affect Sales and Profit, and are higher discounts associated with lower profitability?
6. How does delivery performance vary across Shipping Modes, Regions, and Warehouses?
7. Are returned orders associated with lower customer ratings, particular products, categories, or regions?
8. How do Sales and Profit change over time, and are there noticeable monthly trends or periods of unusually strong or weak performance?

## Key Findings

### 1. Overall Business Performance

SmartKart generated ₹172.45M in sales and ₹22.34M in profit from 5,000 transactions, giving an overall profit margin of 13%. A total of 17,424 units were sold across 12 products to 600 unique customers.

### 2. Product and Category Performance

Electronics is the main sales and profit contributor, but its major products have relatively low margins. Laptop 15 generated the highest sales but had only an 8% margin, while Smart TV had a 7% margin.

In contrast, Face Wash and Water Bottle had much higher margins of 55% and 51%, but their sales were much lower. This shows that high sales do not always mean high profitability.

### 3. Regional Performance

South generated the highest sales, while West had the highest profit margin at 15%. East had the lowest regional margin at 11%.

The lower margin in East appears to be influenced by its heavy dependence on Electronics, where margins are relatively low.

### 4. Customer and Product Mix

Female customers generated slightly higher overall sales and profit than male customers.

Female customers purchased more Galaxy Buds, while male customers purchased more Smart TVs. This shows that differences in product mix can also affect profitability between customer groups.

### 5. Impact of Discounts

Profit margin declines as discount levels increase.

The overall margin drops from 20% at 0–5% discount to just 2% at 16–20% discount. Electronics and Grocery also showed negative margins within the 16–20% discount range.

### 6. Delivery Performance

Delivery times are fairly consistent across regions, with all regions averaging around 5 days.

MUM_WH performed slightly better at around 4 days, while Express shipping also averaged around 4 days. The Same Day shipping label requires further verification because actual delivery duration ranges from 1–8 days.

### 7. Returns and Customer Ratings

The overall return rate is relatively low at 2.7%.

Returned and non-returned transactions have similar average customer ratings of around 3/5, so no strong relationship between returns and ratings was identified.

### 8. Monthly Performance

May recorded the lowest profit margin at 6%, despite generating the second-highest monthly sales.

Smart TV and Laptop 15 together contributed around 88% of May's sales, while both products had relatively low margins.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

1. Review pricing, costs and discount levels for high-revenue, low-margin products.

2. Control excessive discounting, especially in the higher discount ranges.

3. Explore ways to increase sales of high-margin products while maintaining their margins.

4. Investigate the weaker profitability of the East region and its product mix.

5. Monitor monthly profit margin alongside sales instead of focusing only on revenue.
